Before you begin

Keep your details ready and use clear, readable images if you choose to upload documents.

  • Use your legal name.
  • Choose the country that issued your ID.
  • Make sure both ID sides are readable.
  • Do not share passwords, PINs or bank login details.
  • Submitting an enquiry does not guarantee approval.

Help centre

Frequently asked questions

Who can submit an enquiry?

People may submit an enquiry from a listed country. Eligibility and programme availability must be confirmed by the programme administrator.

Are my ID images uploaded to this website?

This single-page website previews selected images in your browser and does not send them to a server. If you choose to share them, you must attach them yourself in WhatsApp.

Does submitting a form guarantee support?

No. An enquiry is not an approval or guarantee of funding. Confirm programme details and the identity of the organisation before sharing sensitive documents.
