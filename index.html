IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Privacy & Terms

Review these notices before using the application form. They are a starter template and should be checked against the organization's real practices and applicable Zambian law.

Privacy Policy

Information collected: the form may collect your name, NRC number, telephone number, district, selected program, requested amount and optional description.

Purpose: these details are used to prepare the enquiry message you choose to send. This static website does not itself have a database or a server that verifies identities.

WhatsApp: if you confirm, the website places the information in a WhatsApp message link. WhatsApp and the receiving account may process that information under their own terms. The message is not sent until you press Send in WhatsApp.

Security: avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information. Do not provide passwords, PINs, banking credentials or one-time codes. Do not publish real applicants' NRC details.

Before launch: add the organization's genuine privacy contact, retention policy, data controller details and process for exercising data rights.

Terms & Conditions

  • This page is an information and enquiry interface, not a funding contract or approval letter.
  • Any program must have real funding, published eligibility criteria and an authorized decision-maker.
  • The requested amount is not a promise that money will be awarded or available.
  • Applicants should provide accurate information and may be asked to follow a verified, lawful assessment process.
  • No payment is requested or collected by this page. Never pay an individual claiming to guarantee approval.
  • The organization must publish its verified legal identity and contact details before representing the website as an official registered service.
